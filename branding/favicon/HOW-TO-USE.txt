Next.js (App Router): copy favicon.ico, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png into /app or /public.

Plain HTML <head>:
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">

favicon.svg switches automatically between light and dark browser themes.
favicon.ico / PNGs use the green tile so they are visible on any tab colour.
